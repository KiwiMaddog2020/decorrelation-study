# Corpus manifest (confirmatory set: snippets 01-20)

Snippet `001` is the smoke pilot (obvious defects, runs/smoke). The study
corpus is `01`-`20`, hand-written realistic Python with planted defects and
exact ground truth in `corpus/truth/NN.json`.

## Shape

- 20 snippets: **15 defective + 5 clean**.
- **24 planted defects**, balanced across the seven types:
  api-misuse 4, boundary 4, logic 4, security 4, concurrency 3,
  spec-violation 3, performance 2 (performance is the lightest cell, so its
  per-type result is the weakest-powered).
- Difficulty: **22 subtle + 2 obvious anchors**. The anchors (05-d2 off-by-one,
  11-d3 SQL injection) are deliberate calibration points: we expect every
  reviewer to catch them, as a sanity check and a contrast for the subtle ones.
- Clean controls (16-20): truncate, chunk, deep-merge, sort, safe-path-join.
  Zero planted defects, for an honest false-positive measurement.

## Difficulty discipline

Subtle = a competent reviewer skimming the function would plausibly miss it:
a non-obvious interaction, a boundary value, a plausible-but-wrong API choice,
or a quiet mismatch with the documented contract. Textbook defects were
forbidden except where explicitly tagged `obvious`. The obvious-defect smoke
test (snippet 001) is the evidence for this: identical, textbook defects
produced universal agreement and zero decorrelation signal.

## Provenance

1. Generated by 20 independent Claude agents (the planter is Claude, per
   pre-registration limitation #2), one snippet each.
2. Adversarially self-checked by 20 separate clean Claude agents (doer != checker):
   are the planted defects actually present at the claimed lines, genuinely
   subtle, free of unintended obvious bugs, and are the clean snippets truly
   clean? 7 accepted outright, 13 flagged. See `runs/gen_selfcheck.md`.
3. Reconciled against disk by the orchestrator: 9 snippets had mis-counted line
   anchors corrected; 4 needed code fixes (05 raise-None crash guarded, 06
   non-biting rounding made real, 15 int() truncation removed, 16 over-promising
   docstring narrowed). `harness/align_report.py` confirms every cited line is
   live code.

## Open verification steps (not yet done)

  model from the Claude planter), per pre-registration: find ALL defects,

- **Human reconciliation** (Kevin): the reconciled list is the ground truth of
  record. Until then this ground truth is provisional.

## Known scoring nuance

A few snippets carry co-located defects of different types on the same lines
(e.g. 01 logic+performance, 11 transaction-leak+state-leak). The scorer
(`harness/score.py`) breaks these ties by type/content affinity rather than
list order; genuinely ambiguous reviewer flags are human-adjudicated per
pre-registration amendment 1.
